Why I moved my side projects to Cloudflare Workers

Gartha
[DATE] 8 min read

For years every side project started the same way: pick a VPS, harden it, forget about it, then find out six months later that the box has been unpatched the whole time. I wanted the fun part without the ops part.

Workers gave me that. The site, the API, the cron job that reads my inbox every morning, and the database all live in one account and deploy with one command.

The whole deploy is one file

Here is the entire config. Static assets for the Astro build, a D1 binding, and a cron trigger for the daily report.

wrangler.toml 
name = "gartha-me"
main = "apps/api/src/index.ts"

[assets]
directory = "apps/web/dist"

[[d1_databases]]
binding = "DB"

[triggers]
crons = ["0 22 * * *"]

That cron line is the whole scheduler. No systemd, no container, no "is the box still up" anxiety.
